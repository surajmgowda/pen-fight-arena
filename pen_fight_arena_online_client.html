Rules

How to play

Touch only your own pen. Pull in the opposite direction from where you want it to travel. Release to flick. The contact point matters: an off-centre hit can rotate or redirect a pen, while a central hit produces a stronger straight push. Wait until every moving pen settles before the next turn. If the pen crosses the table boundary, it is out.
